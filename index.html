Winter Recommendations

Looking for the perfect winter facial that supports skin renewal?

RX Facials are progressive treatments, rather than purely aggressive ones. It is a common misconception that peels must show visible flaking or scabbing to be effective but this is not true for the majority of people seeking rejuvenation. We aim to infuse the skin with beneficial and nourishing ingredients that allow your skin to reach optimal health levels and the best environment for cell renewal.

All RX Facials Include
  • A Skin Care Consultation
  • A Deep Cleanse
  • An Exfoliating Treatment and Extractions
  • A High Performance Mask
  • RX-Elite Deep Pore Cleanse
  • A Prescribed RX-Elite Mask
  • Speciality Eye Cream and Antioxidant Serum
  • SPF Sunscreen if required
  • A Complimentary Beer or Wine

RX Benefactor Facial

$170 · 1 Hour

This mask is suitable for problem skin, including conditions associated with premature aging, photo-damage, hyper-pigmentation and scarring. The benefits include aiding in cell renewal, softening of the skin, and lightening and brightening the complexion.

RX Illuminate Facial (NEW)

$170 · 1 Hour

This renewing and brightening treatment combines the power of Vitamin A and Vitamin C into one illuminating facial treatment. Featuring a mild retinol complex and a vitamin C-infused peel, it revitalises tired, lacklustre skin while encouraging the growth of healthy, youthful skin cells. A soothing facial massage, followed by a hydrating alginate mask and protective SPF, completes the experience.

RX Detox Facial

$189 · 1 Hour

The RX Detox Facial is ideal for problematic skin that experiences redness, enlarged pores, mild scarring and general toxicity issues. The peel is beneficial for these conditions by helping to exfoliate, mattify, detoxify and calming down the overall nature of the skin.
